# DRAFT — How much does a small business website cost?

A small business website quote is a decision about **scope**, not a price for a number of pixels. A five-page informational site, an online store and a customer portal solve different problems. Before comparing quotes, write down what visitors must learn and what action they should take: call, send a WhatsApp message, request a quote, book an appointment or buy a product.

## What usually changes the quote?

**Pages and content.** “Five pages” is useful only when you know what goes on each page. A service page needing original copy, comparison tables and custom imagery takes more work than a supplied short text page. Decide who writes the copy and provides usable images.

**Design and functionality.** A unique visual direction, complex navigation, booking flow, customer account or product filter should be listed as a requirement. An online store also needs decisions about catalog data, shipping, taxes and payment providers. A static brochure site does not need those systems.

**Integrations and editing.** If staff must update content themselves, specify what they need to change and whether an editing system is required. A contact form, CRM connection, newsletter integration and automation each have different implementation and maintenance implications. Do not assume they are included because a quote says “website.”

**Handoff and ongoing costs.** Ask whether domain registration, hosting, paid fonts or stock imagery, gateway fees, maintenance and future changes are included. NEXT Studio lists these separately unless a written proposal says otherwise. Revision rounds, final file access and launch checks should also be agreed before work begins.

## A brief that leads to a useful quotation

Send five things: (1) what the business offers, (2) who the site is for, (3) the most important visitor action, (4) the pages and assets already available, and (5) any deadline or required integration. If you have an existing site, include its URL and the specific parts that are not working for visitors. A good quote should answer the scope and exclusions, not merely repeat a package label.
