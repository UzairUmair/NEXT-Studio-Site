## Owner review rhythm

Every 2–4 weeks, spend roughly 30–45 minutes checking Page indexing reasons and exporting Performance dimensions. Ask buyers what made them inquire and privately tally qualified WhatsApp messages. Keep names, phone numbers and message content out of Git. Do not present a prepared WhatsApp draft as a sent lead.
