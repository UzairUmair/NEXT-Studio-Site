## Content and authority implications

- PK: A concrete WhatsApp inquiry walkthrough, PKR package boundaries and a real responsive example are more defensible than a generic “best agency” claim. The owner should confirm delivery and support terms before adding them.
- US: Pricing competitors show detailed inclusions, hosting, ownership and maintenance. NEXT Studio's prices are starting points, not exact fixed contracts; explain that difference and demonstrate build choices through honest personal projects.
- SA: Local agencies have Arabic content, regional payment integrations, physical locations and portfolio proof. No Saudi page, hreflang or Google Business Profile is justified from the current evidence. Reassess if real Saudi inquiries appear.
- Across markets: freelancer and concept work are valuable process evidence, but do not equal commissioned outcomes or reviews. Publish only original, fact-checked resources. Earn links through genuine portfolio sharing and relationships, with no paid or reciprocal link scheme.

These observations complement `05-competitor-serp-notes.md`; they do not retroactively validate the examples listed in the owner's strategy document as current ranking positions.
