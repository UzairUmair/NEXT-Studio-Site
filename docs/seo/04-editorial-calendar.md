# 90-day Google growth calendar — $0 incremental spend

Window: 20 October 2026–18 January 2027. Suggested effort: roughly 2–4 focused owner hours per week for review, accurate examples and inquiry follow-up, plus development time already available. Publish only after owner review. No paid tools, ads, backlinks, domains or outsourced content.

| When | Small deliverable | Evidence and decision gate |
|---|---|---|
| Days 1–7 | Audit technical crawlability, build keyword-intent map, clarify homepage and buyer service copy, strengthen honest project proof. | Local build and browser checks; owner approval before production deployment. |
| Weeks 2–4 | Review Search Console coverage and first query/page rows if present; adjust one weak service section using real buyer questions. Review Pure Roots claims and portfolio image permissions. | GSC export or “Processing data” screenshot; owner facts. |
| Week 3 or 4 | Review draft **How much does a small business website cost?** against current packages, hosting and revision terms. If accurate, publish one substantial resource with a clear service/pricing link. | Draft in `drafts/website-cost.md`; publishing approval required. |
| Month 2, weeks 5–8 | Review draft **Website or web application?** with CineVerse and FreelancerFlow examples. Publish only if the owner confirms the examples and demo scope. Add a genuine project write-up to an owned profile if desired. | Draft in `drafts/website-vs-web-app.md`; no mass production or automated posting. |
| Month 2 | Repeat 28-day Search Console and manual inquiry review. Improve one page with impressions but weak click-through or an intent mismatch; leave pages without data alone unless usability evidence warrants a fix. | `monthly-scorecard.csv` and source notes. |
| Month 3, weeks 9–12 | Refresh the strongest resource or one service page based on actual questions; inspect indexed/not-indexed reasons and real-user CWV if available. Prepare one human-approved relevant showcase/outreach opportunity. | Before/after page notes, direct GSC data and owner feedback. |
| Day 90 | Compare equivalent periods, separate branded from nonbranded demand, review received qualified inquiries and produce the next-quarter priority list. | No “ranked #1” or ROI statement without verified measurement. |

Editorial pace: at most one substantial resource in month 1 and one in month 2, followed by an evidence-based update in month 3. The two local drafts are review materials, not published pages or sitemap entries. [Google's people-first content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) favors original usefulness over scaled search-targeted text.
